Vote: Better Together

Delete your account

You can delete your Vote account and its data at any time, in the app or by e-mail. Here’s how, and exactly what happens.

Version 2026-10-06Applies to the Vote app by Vote: Better Together

Just need a break? You can pause your account instead, for 1 day, 3 days, a week or until you turn it back on. Pausing hides you from Discover, pairs and introductions, and your existing chats keep working.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Vote and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account. Vote offers to pause instead; choose to continue.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you’re signed out. Want a copy first? Use Settings › Your data › Export my data before you delete.

Can’t open the app? Ask by e-mail

Write to ahmetfceren@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Vote account”. Send it from the e-mail address of your Vote account. If you signed in with Apple or Google, tell us which one and the e-mail address of that account.

To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request before we delete anything. We delete the account as soon as it’s confirmed, no later than 30 days after your request, and let you know by e-mail when it’s done.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes it from Vote’s database at once, including:

  • your sign-in account, profile, settings and all your photos
  • your likes, passes and notes, your reads of pairs, and the pairs you follow or propose
  • your matches, introductions and the conversations you were part of, with the photos sent in them
  • your birth details and Daily Orbits, and your answers after a date
  • your blocks, notifications and the push token of your devices
  • your support requests and the reports you made that are closed

What is kept, and for how long

  • Open safety reports. A report still open when you delete your account, one you made or one about you, is kept with no link to any account until Vote’s safety team closes it, together with a chat photo it concerns, so a safety review can finish.
  • Usage counts. Counts of how the app was used lose their link to you and are removed after 13 months.
  • Purchase records. If you ever subscribed, records of App Store purchase events are kept with no link to your account for up to 400 days, for accounting and to prevent fraud.
  • Backups. Copies may remain in the hosting provider’s backups until those expire.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Vote account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.
