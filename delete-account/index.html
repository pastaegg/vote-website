Deleting your account and data

In the app at any time, or by e-mail if you can’t sign in.

Delete your account in the app

Open Vote, then Settings › Delete account, and confirm. You need to be signed in, and the deletion happens at once. It can’t be undone, so if you want a copy of your data, save it first with Settings › Your data › “Export my data”. If you only want a break, you can pause your profile instead.

What is deleted, and when

At once and for good: your account and sign-in, profile, photos, likes and notes, reads, follows and the pairs you proposed, matches, the conversations you were part of and their photos (the other person loses them too), introductions, blocks, your settings and consents, your support requests and the reports you made that are closed. If you signed in with Apple on an iPhone, Vote also asks Apple to revoke Vote’s access to your Apple ID, and keeps trying every hour if Apple can’t be reached. Copies in our hosting provider’s backups are gone when those backups expire, after a short period.

What is kept, and why

A few things stay, only as long as needed:
• If your account was banned: one-way hashes (SHA-256) of its e-mail address, sign-in identities and install IDs stay on the ban list until the ban is lifted, so the same person can’t simply start again. Nobody can read an address or an ID from them.
• A report still open, one you made or one about you, and a chat photo it concerns: kept without any link to an account until Vote’s safety team closes it.
• Records of moderation decisions: kept so moderation stays accountable, without the link to your account.
• Usage statistics: they lose their link to you and are deleted after 13 months.
• What the App Store reported about a subscription: up to 400 days.
• Legal holds: if the law requires us to keep something, for example under a court order or a lawful request from an authority, or we need it to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim, we keep only that, only as long as required, and then delete it.

If you can’t open the app

Write to us from the e-mail address of your Vote account, or tell us how you sign in (Apple, Google or e-mail) and the first name on your profile, and ask us to delete your account. We may ask you to confirm that it is you, so that nobody can delete someone else’s account. We delete it within 30 days at the latest, usually much sooner, and confirm by e-mail.
E-mail: support@votebettertogether.com

Subscriptions and signing in again

Deleting your Vote account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Apple sign-ins on Android, or from before this version of Vote, can’t be revoked by our servers: you can remove Vote in your Apple ID settings. If you later sign in again with the same Apple, Google or e-mail account, a new, empty account starts, unless that account was banned.
