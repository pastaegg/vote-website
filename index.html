How it works

Read a pair. Say what you see. They decide the rest.

Sometimes another person notices a connection you would have passed by. On Vote, people read possible pairs. Their reads can lead to an introduction, and the choice always stays with the two people.

The pairs themselves are not random. Vote’s matching system looks for two people whose preferences fit both ways and who seem to have something in common, with a little room for surprise, and then people read them. Reads from accounts that look coordinated count for less, so no group can steer a pair.

  1. Read a pair

    Two people who could plausibly want each other, side by side. Drag the Orb up if you can see them together, down if you can’t.

  2. Say what you see

    Chemistry, values, vibe, personality, style. Each one counts on its own. There is no single score, and nobody is rated as a person.

  3. The community reads too

    Your read joins everyone else’s. Reads are anonymous: nobody learns who read a pair, and a pass is never shown.

  4. Both say yes

    When enough people see the same thing, the two get an introduction. A chat opens only if both of them say yes.

Inside the app

A closer look at Vote.

Vote

“Can you see these two together?” Answer with the Orb or the buttons, or say you can’t tell. Then say what you see, and imagine their first date if you like. Your pairs shows the pairs you are in, one card each, with nothing about anyone else. You can also propose two people you think should meet.

Discover

One person at a time. Like a photo or an answer, with a comment if you want, or pass. When you both like each other, a chat opens. Discover is for people who are looking. Anyone can read pairs.

Chats and Community

Chats holds your matches and introductions. From the menu, Community lets you follow people and talk in rooms, with a profile kept apart from your dating profile.

Your profile

Your profile and who sees what. Preview it as others do, choose the photos readers can see, and take a break whenever you want. While you are away you stay out of Discover and pairs, and you can still read pairs and keep your chats.

Your sky is there too, just for fun.

Privacy controls

Choose what you share.

Set an audience for each part of your profile and choose which photos community readers can see. Read the Privacy Policy for the details and the limits.

Approximate location

Profiles show a city or neighbourhood, and distances are rounded. Your city comes from your phone, and never your exact position.

Community summaries

The app shows community results and counts. It never names who read, proposed or followed your pairs.

Photo audiences

Who can see a photo depends on your settings, and photos are shown through links that expire soon. A photo already on someone’s screen can stay visible for a while.

A quiet website

This website uses its own fonts and images. No cookies, no advertising, no analytics.

Here to help

You don’t have to date to take part. Adults can read pairs without a dating profile.

Your data

Export your data or delete your account in Settings. Deletion instructions explain the email option and what may be kept.

Safety

A no is final. Be kind.

Every pair is two real people. Vote asks everyone to be honest and considerate. The community guidelines are part of joining, and reporting and support are there if something goes wrong.

  • Both people say yesCommunity reads never connect anyone on their own.
  • Report and blockUse the safety menu on a profile, pair, introduction or chat, or write to support.
  • Kind wordsVote checks for some harmful language and asks you to think again about words that may hurt. Report anything that makes you feel unsafe.
  • A way to ask for helpSettings has Help & support and a way to appeal account restrictions. You can reach Vote by email too.
  • For adultsVote is for people 18 and older and asks for a birth date during setup. Members must use their real age.
