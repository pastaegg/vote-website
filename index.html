How it works

Read a pair. Say what you see. Then see the community’s read.

Your friends have always been good at spotting who you’d click with. Vote brings that to dating: a community of people who read pairs honestly, and anonymously.

  1. Read a pair

    Two real members, side by side. Can you see them together?

  2. Say what you see

    A spark, they’d work, meant to meet. Or not seeing it. Pick the lenses: chemistry, vibe, style, personality, spirit.

  3. See the community’s read

    Results appear only after you read, and only once at least five people have read the pair. Never a single score.

  4. It can become an introduction

    When enough people see the same thing, the two of them are introduced. Both have to say yes.

  5. You get read too

    Others read you with people who fit your preferences. You see anonymous counts, never who read you.

Privacy by design

Seen by the community. Never exposed.

Readers see only the photos and profile sections you choose. The rest stays between you and your connections.

Approximate location

Vote shows your city, never your coordinates, and rounds your location to about 1 km. Or just type your city.

Anonymous reads

Nobody sees who read, proposed or followed them. Only counts, and only once five people have read a pair.

Private photos

Photos are stored privately. Each link to one works for a few minutes, and only while your settings allow it.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no ad identifiers, no analytics companies. Vote never asks for your contacts.

No AI writing about you

Astrology readings are texts Vote wrote in advance, chosen by its own calculation. Nothing about you is sent to an AI.

Your data, your call

Export everything Vote keeps about you, pause your account, or delete it from Settings at any time.

Safety

A no is final. Kindness isn’t optional.

Every pair is two real people. Reads are about fit, never about ranking anyone. The community guidelines are part of joining, and real people enforce them.

  • Both people say yesCommunity reads never connect anyone on their own.
  • Report and block, everywhereFrom any profile, pair, introduction or chat. Reports are confidential, and a block works both ways, at once.
  • Kind words“Are you sure?” before words that may hurt. Threats, slurs and telling someone to hurt themselves are never sent.
  • People review reportsReports and appeals are reviewed by a person on Vote’s team, not decided automatically.
  • 18+ onlyYour birth date is checked when you join. One account per person.
