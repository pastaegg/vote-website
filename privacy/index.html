Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vote: Better Together ("Vote", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use our app, website and related services.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile information

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, dating or discovery preferences, intentions, profile text, photos and other information you choose to add to your profile.

Location information

We may use your device location or a location you provide to support nearby discovery. Where possible, the service may display approximate rather than precise location information to other users.

Activity and interaction data

We may collect information about how you use Vote, including profiles you view, community votes or reads, follows, matches, chats, settings, reports, blocks and other interactions with the service.

Authentication and device information

If you sign in using Apple, Google or another authentication provider, we receive information permitted by that provider. We may also collect device, operating system, app version, log and security information needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, personalize and maintain Vote.
  • Enable discovery, community voting, matching, messaging and related features.
  • Protect users, prevent abuse, investigate reports and enforce our rules.
  • Improve reliability, product quality and user experience.
  • Communicate with you about your account, security or service updates.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. What other users may see

Depending on your settings and the product feature, other users may see profile information such as your name, photos, age or age range, approximate location, profile details, community participation and other information you choose to make visible.

4. Messages and community content

Content you send or submit through Vote may be stored so the service can deliver the feature, maintain safety, respond to reports and comply with legal requirements.

5. Service providers

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, authentication, database services, analytics, notifications, security, customer support and other infrastructure. These providers may process information on our behalf subject to their contractual obligations.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers, when you direct us to do so, to protect users or the service, in connection with a business transaction, or where required by law.

7. Data retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including operating the service, maintaining security, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations.

8. Your choices and controls

Depending on the feature and your location, you may be able to update your profile, change privacy and discovery settings, download certain account information, pause your account, or request deletion of your account and associated information.

9. Children

Vote is intended only for people age 18 or older. We do not knowingly permit people under 18 to create or use an account.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we take steps intended to provide appropriate safeguards for that processing.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date when changes are made.
