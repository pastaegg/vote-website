Getting help

Open Vote, then Settings › Help & support. You can ask about your account, privacy and your data, safety, or anything else. A person on Vote’s team answers there, in the app.

How reading pairs works

You see two people side by side and say whether you can see them together, then what you notice between them. When enough people see the same thing, the two get an introduction, and they talk only if both say yes. Reads are anonymous, and nobody is told about a pass. If both say yes, the people who voted on or follow the pair may see that they gave each other a chance, and later that they met or are still talking if both share how their dates go. Either of them can stop this at once with “Leave introductions” in Settings › Looking for someone.

Why you don’t see someone

Vote shows people whose preferences and yours fit both ways. Someone may also be taking a break, not looking, too far away, or there may be a block between you, and Vote never says which.

Photos in review

A new photo shows “In review” until a person on Vote’s team has checked it. Others see it once it is accepted. If it is turned down, you are told, and the Community guidelines list what is turned down.

Blocking

A block works at once and both ways: you stop seeing each other in Discover, pairs, introductions, chats and Community. You can unblock someone in Settings › Blocked. You may then see each other again in Discover, but a match, chat or introduction the block ended stays closed.

Appeals

If your account was suspended or restricted and you think it was a mistake, open Vote: the screen your account shows has “Appeal or ask for help”. Your appeal is reviewed by a person, not decided automatically.

Reporting someone

On any profile, pair, introduction or chat, open the menu and choose Report. Reports are confidential: the person is never told who reported them.

Your data and privacy

For a copy of your data, use Settings › Your data › “Export my data”. To delete your account, use Settings › Delete account. For any other privacy request, write to us in the app under Settings › Help & support (“Privacy and my data”). Our Privacy Policy explains your rights.

If you are in danger

Contact local emergency services first. Vote cannot send help.

Can’t sign in

Use the sign-in method you chose when you joined (Apple, Google or e-mail). With e-mail, “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen sends a reset link.

Contact

If you can’t open the app: support@votebettertogether.com

Staying safe

Keep it on Vote

Chat here until you’re comfortable. Be wary of anyone who rushes you off the app.

Meet in public

For first dates, choose a public place and tell a friend where you’ll be.

Never send money

Report anyone who asks for money, gift cards or crypto.

Trust your instincts

Use the safety menu to unmatch, block or report a concern, or email support if you cannot use the app.

If you can’t use the app

Write to support@votebettertogether.com. Send it from the e-mail address of your Vote account if you can, or tell us how you sign in (Apple, Google or e-mail), so we can find your account.
