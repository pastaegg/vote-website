Vote is a dating app where other people notice, and the two of you decide.

  1. Vote on a pair

    Two people side by side, one question: can you see these two together? Drag the Orb up or down, then say what you notice: chemistry, values, vibe, personality or style.

  2. The community notices

    Votes are anonymous and always about the pair. When enough people see the same thing, those two get a private introduction. You are in pairs too.

  3. You both decide

    A conversation opens only when both of you say yes. Nobody is connected without it.

Prefer to look for yourself? Discover shows one person at a time. Like a photo or an answer, and a chat opens when you both like each other.

Nothing starts without both of you.

Vote shows what other people notice. It never tells you who is right for you, and the decision stays with you.

  • Two yeses, every timeCommunity votes never connect anyone on their own. A chat opens only when both people say yes.
  • Anonymous votesNobody learns who voted on a pair, and a pass is never shown.
  • Pairs, not scoresA vote is about two people together. No desirability scores, no rankings of people, no compatibility percentages.
  • Your city, never your locationYour birthday and exact distance stay private. You choose who sees each part of your profile.
  • People check new photosA person on Vote’s team checks every new profile photo before anyone else sees it. Report or block from any profile, pair, introduction or chat.
  • Free, for adultsVote is for people 18 and over. It shows a few labeled ads, never chosen from your profile.
