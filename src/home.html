How it works

Read a pair. Say what you see. Then see the community’s read.

Sometimes another person notices a connection you might miss. In Vote, a “read” is your vote on a potential couple. It expresses an opinion, and the choice to connect stays with them.

  1. Read a pair

    Two real members, side by side. Can you see them together?

  2. Say what you see

    Vote together or not seeing it, or skip if you can’t tell. You can add what you noticed from the profile details the pair has shared.

  3. See the community’s read

    After your vote, a community summary appears once enough people have voted. Any percentages reflect community opinion, not a measure of romantic compatibility.

  4. They choose what’s next

    Community votes can lead to an introduction. A romantic connection and chat begin only when both people accept.

  5. Date, help, or both

    People in Discover can vote too. If you’re only here to help, complete the reader setup and vote without appearing in Discover or in pairs.

Inside the app

A closer look at Vote.

Vote

“Can you see these two together?” Vote using the Orb or the buttons, or skip with “Can’t tell”. You can add what you noticed and imagine a first date. Pair proposals and following are available when enabled for your account and allowed by the people involved.

Discover

One profile at a time. Like, pass, or send a like with a note. Two mutual likes make a match and open a chat. Discover is for people who are looking and have completed their dating profile; community voting is open to eligible members who aren’t looking too.

Connections

Your introductions, likes and conversations. Accepting an introduction takes two yeses. Chat with a mutual connection, share photos, and choose your read receipt settings.

You

Your profile and its audiences. Preview what others can see, choose the photos shared with community readers, and manage whether you’re looking for someone in Settings. Taking a break or choosing “Not looking” keeps you out of Discover and pairs while you can still vote and keep existing chats.

Plus Daily Orbit: a daily astrology reading, just for you.

Privacy controls

Choose what you share.

Set an audience for each profile section and choose which photos community readers can see. Read the Privacy Policy for details and limits.

Approximate location

Profiles show a city or neighbourhood, with rounded distances where enabled. You can type your city instead of using your phone’s location.

Community summaries

The app shows community results and counts without naming the members who voted on, proposed or followed your pairs.

Photo audiences

Photo access depends on your settings and uses short-lived links. An issued link or a photo already on someone’s screen can remain visible for a time.

A quiet website

This website uses local fonts and images, with no cookies, advertising or analytics scripts.

Here to help

You don’t have to date to participate. The helper setup lets eligible adults read pairs without creating a dating profile.

Manage your data

Export your data or request account deletion from Settings. Deletion instructions explain the email option and data that may be retained.

Safety

A no is final. Kindness isn’t optional.

Each pair represents two members. Vote asks people to be honest and considerate. The community guidelines are part of joining, with reporting and support available if something goes wrong.

  • Both people say yesCommunity reads never connect anyone on their own.
  • Report and blockUse the safety menu on a profile, pair, introduction or chat. You can also contact support about a concern.
  • Kind wordsVote checks for certain harmful language and asks you to reconsider words that may hurt. Report content that makes you feel unsafe.
  • A way to ask for helpSettings includes Help & support and an appeal route for account restrictions. You can reach Vote by email too.
  • For adultsVote requires members to be 18 or older and asks for a birth date during setup. Members must use their real age.
