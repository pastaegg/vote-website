Community reads

Reads on pairs are opinions, not facts. They never connect anyone without both people saying yes. A read, a percentage or an introduction is not a promise that two people will get along, or that anyone is safe to meet.

Using Vote fairly

Don’t use Vote to harass, threaten, deceive or exploit anyone, to ask for money or sell anything, or for anyone under 18. Vote does not run criminal background checks and does not verify who members are. You are responsible for your own interactions, on Vote and off it. Take care when you talk to or meet someone: keep it on Vote until you are comfortable, meet in public, and never send money. If something goes wrong, report it in the app and, if you are in danger, contact local emergency services.

Plans

Vote is free. Vote Plus and Vote Max add features such as unlimited likes and votes, undo, seeing who likes you and your full vote history. What your plan includes is decided by Vote’s servers. A plan never changes how the community reads pairs or what the percentages mean, and privacy settings, blocking and reporting are the same on every plan. On any plan, Vote may limit automated or abusive use.

Deleting your Vote account does not cancel an App Store subscription or a Google Play subscription: cancel it with the store.

Community guidelines

Be real

Use your own photos and your real age. No impersonation.

Read generously

Every pair is two real people. Reads are about fit, never about ranking anyone.

A no is final

Don’t pressure anyone to accept a like, a match or an introduction.

Zero tolerance

Harassment, hate, threats, scams and sexual content without consent lead to removal.

Kind words

Vote never sends or shows threats of sexual violence, words telling someone to kill themselves, or slurs, and asks “Are you sure?” before words that may hurt. If a message bothers you, report it: a person on Vote’s team reviews it.
