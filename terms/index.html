How Vote works

In Discover you can like someone, like them with a note or pass, and two likes make a match. In community Vote, members read pairs of other people and say whether the two make sense together. Reads are opinions, not facts. A pair’s result is shown only after at least five people have read it, as a share rounded to 5% with a word such as “Most” or “Few”, and it belongs to the pair, never to one person. Once you have seen a pair’s result, your read on that pair is final. When readers see something strong, Vote may introduce the two people, and they are connected only if both say yes. Astrology in Vote is for fun only and never decides anything. Vote does not promise matches, introductions, dates or relationships.

Community guidelines

Be real

Use your own photos and your real age. One person, one account. No impersonation.

Read generously

Every pair is two real people. Reads are about fit, never about ranking anyone or judging looks alone.

A no is final

Don’t pressure anyone to accept a like, a match or an introduction.

Keep private things private

Don’t share anyone’s messages, photos or details outside Vote without their consent, and never reveal anyone’s sexual orientation or gender identity.

Zero tolerance

Harassment, hate, threats, scams and sexual content without consent lead to removal.

Kind words

Vote never sends or shows threats of sexual violence, words telling someone to kill themselves, or slurs, and asks “Are you sure?” before words that may hurt. If a message bothers you, report it: a person on Vote’s team reviews it.
