How Vote works

In Discover you can like someone or one of their photos or answers, with a comment if you want, or pass, and two likes make a match. In community Vote, members read pairs of other people and say whether the two make sense together. Reads are opinions, not facts. A pair’s result is shown only after at least five people have read it, as a share rounded to 5% with a word such as “Most” or “Few”, and it belongs to the pair, never to one person. Once at least ten other people have read a pair you read, you also see the exact share of them who answered as you did. Once you have seen a pair’s result, your read on that pair is final. When readers see something strong, Vote may introduce the two people, and they are connected only if both say yes. Astrology in Vote is for fun only and never decides anything. Vote does not promise matches, introductions, dates or relationships.

Paid plans

Vote is free to use, with the Vote Free plan. An optional paid plan, Vote Plus, is not on sale yet. When Vote offers it, what it includes and its price are shown in the app before you buy, and Vote’s servers decide what your plan includes. A plan never changes how the community reads pairs or what a result means, and privacy settings, blocking and reporting are the same on every plan. Reading pairs is the same on every plan, with the same limits, such as your latest 50 rated pairs in your history and 15 proposals every 30 days. Vote Plus adds features only in Discover and in chats, such as more likes, likes with a note, read receipts and more photos in chat. Safety and fair-use limits still apply.

Community guidelines

Be real

Use your own photos and your real age. One person, one account. No impersonation.

Your photos

Use photos of you. Your first photo shows your face clearly. A person on Vote’s team checks every new photo before anyone else sees it, and turns one down when it shows:
• nudity or sexual content
• anyone under 18, even with you
• someone else, or no way to tell that it is you
• phone numbers, usernames, QR codes or links
• ID cards, bank cards or other documents
• weapons, drugs, violence, blood or self harm
• hate symbols

Read generously

Every pair is two real people. Reads are about fit, never about ranking anyone or judging looks alone.

Read honestly

Say what you really see. If you know someone in a pair, choose “Can’t tell”. Don’t read a pair to punish or to help anyone, don’t agree with others on how to read one, and don’t post screenshots of pairs outside Vote.

A no is final

Don’t pressure anyone to accept a like, a match or an introduction.

Respect boundaries

No reply, an unmatch or a block means no: don’t contact the person another way or look for them on other apps. Don’t send sexual photos or messages nobody asked for. How you treat someone when you meet counts too, and you can report what happened on a date.

Keep private things private

Don’t share anyone’s messages, photos or details outside Vote without their consent, and never reveal anyone’s sexual orientation or gender identity.

Zero tolerance

Harassment, hate, threats, scams and sexual content without consent lead to removal.

Kind words

Vote’s servers refuse threats of sexual violence, words telling someone to kill themselves and slurs on a short list, and the app asks “Are you sure?” before words that may hurt. No filter catches everything: if a message bothers you, report it, and a person on Vote’s team reviews it.

Be genuine in Community

Don’t pretend to be someone else, mislead people about who you are, or run accounts to manipulate follows, posts, reports or reading.

Respect people and their privacy

No threats, targeted harassment or hate. Never share someone else’s photos, private messages, contact details, precise location or dating profile without their consent.

Share only what you may

No sexual content involving anyone under 18, no intimate images shared without consent, no scams or unlawful material. No nudity or sexually explicit content in Community.

Keep Community useful

No spam, bulk soliciting or deceptive links. Disagree kindly, and don’t use posts or rooms to pressure anyone or to steer how pairs are read.

Report honestly

Reports are for real concerns. Reporting someone to harm them, or to settle a disagreement, breaks these guidelines.

Report concerns

Use report and block. People on Vote’s team review what is reported and act in proportion, and you are told when something of yours is removed. You can ask for a review of a decision through Settings › Help & support.
